## What is AllowMe?

AllowMe is a straightforward allowance tracker for parents. Add your children, set their allowance schedules, log payouts and spending, and keep a full history of every transaction. It's the ledger you used to keep in your head, now on your phone.

## Features

- Add multiple children and manage their allowances independently
- Log payouts, spending, and adjustments with a full transaction history
- See each child's current balance at a glance
- Dark theme support
- Works completely offline — no account, no sync, no internet required

## Privacy

All data stays on your device. AllowMe has no analytics, no advertising, and no server to send your data to.

Free 30-day trial, no credit card required. One-time purchase to unlock permanently after the trial.
