_Last updated: April 2026_

## The short version

Our apps do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. Everything stays on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. Our apps have no analytics, no crash reporting services, no advertising SDKs, and no user accounts. We have no way to identify you, and we have no interest in doing so.

## Data stored on your device

Some apps store data locally on your device (for example, Underspend stores your grocery lists and AllowMe stores your children's allowance history). This data never leaves your device. It is not synced to our servers because we don't have any servers that hold your data.

## Third-party services

Our apps do not integrate with any third-party data services. We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, or similar tools.

## In-app purchases

Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google Play and the Apple App Store. Bloop Studios does not receive or store your payment details.

## Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page and note it in the app release notes.

## Contact

If you have any questions about privacy, email us at [support@bloopstudios.no](mailto:support@bloopstudios.no).
