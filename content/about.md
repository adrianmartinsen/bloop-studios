## Who we are

We are a husband and wife duo who design and build focused, high-quality consumer applications for Android and iOS — tools that solve real, everyday problems without getting in your way.

Every app we ship is designed with the same priorities: clean interfaces, complete privacy, and genuine utility. No subscriptions where they aren't needed. No data harvested. No bloat.

## How we work

Apps are buildt with Flutter, which means our apps run natively on both Android and iOS from a single codebase — without compromising on performance or feel. Every app features a generous free trial, or a free-to-use core featureset so you can try before you buy. That way you know that our apps are the right fit for you.

## Privacy by default

All data in our apps stays on your device. We don't collect it, we don't sell it, and we don't send it anywhere. This isn't a policy we wrote to satisfy a lawyer — it's how the apps are built.
